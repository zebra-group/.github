# zebra | group ♥ Open Source

Welcome to **zebra | group** — a digital (and real) marketing agency with teams in
**Chemnitz** and **Dresden**. Since we use and customize open source software in our
products and solutions, we have a lot to contribute.

Our IT teams work with different tech stacks and philosophies for managing content and
creating digital experiences, which is reflected in the way we and our team members
contribute to OSS.

We are very passionate about giving back more and more to the projects that help us
accomplish our mission, and we look forward to the feedback from the community.

## What you'll find here

**Grav CMS** — plugins connecting [Grav](https://getgrav.org) to
[Directus](https://directus.io), plus search, landing pages and utilities

[`grav-plugin-directus2`](https://github.com/zebra-group/grav-plugin-directus2) ·
[`grav-plugin-directus2-router`](https://github.com/zebra-group/grav-plugin-directus2-router) ·
[`grav-plugin-directus-dynpages`](https://github.com/zebra-group/grav-plugin-directus-dynpages) ·
[`grav-plugin-personio-jobs`](https://github.com/zebra-group/grav-plugin-personio-jobs) ·
[`grav-plugin-algolia-search`](https://github.com/zebra-group/grav-plugin-algolia-search)

**Directus** — extensions and tooling for [Directus](https://directus.io)

[`directus-extension-hash-generator`](https://github.com/zebra-group/directus-extension-hash-generator) ·
[`directus-extension-installer`](https://github.com/zebra-group/directus-extension-installer)

**Infrastructure** — container images we build and run in production

[`docker-apache-php-fpm`](https://github.com/zebra-group/docker-apache-php-fpm) →
`ghcr.io/zebra-group/php-fpm-apache`

Alongside these you'll find our earlier generation of Grav/Directus plugins, kept
available for existing projects.

## Coming from Mindbox?

Mindbox GmbH is now **zebra white GmbH**, part of zebra group, and every repository moved
from `github.com/mindbox` to here. The old URLs redirect, but please update your remotes.

Note that container images under `ghcr.io/mindbox/…` are frozen and no longer rebuilt —
new builds go to `ghcr.io/zebra-group/…`.
